The first time you log in to Kairo, the platform redirects you to the **Diagnostic** screen before you can access your dashboard. This is a one-time, 30-question soft skills and learning style assessment that takes 5–8 minutes to complete.

<Info>
  The quiz runs once. After you submit it, you are redirected to your dashboard and will not see the diagnostic screen again unless you reset your profile from settings.
</Info>

## Why this matters

Your answers feed directly into Kairo's AI plan generation engine. The Python microservice reads your `soft_skills_assessment` record — alongside your Moodle academic progress — every time it generates or refreshes your 6 learning plan cards. Without completing the diagnostic, no personalized plans can be created.

## How the quiz is structured

The 30 questions are grouped into 7 blocks. Each block explores a different dimension of how you learn:

| Block | Focus | What it measures |
|-------|-------|------------------|
| **B1** | Sensory channels | How you absorb information: visually, auditorily, by reading, or hands-on |
| **B2** | Active vs. Reflective | Whether you jump in immediately or think before acting |
| **B3** | Concrete vs. Intuitive | Whether you prefer real examples or abstract ideas |
| **B4** | Sequential vs. Global | Whether you work step-by-step or need the big picture first |
| **B5** | Visual vs. Verbal | Whether diagrams or written descriptions work better for you |
| **B6** | Experience cycle | How you process learning: experiencing, observing, analyzing, or experimenting |
| **B7** | Integrative profile | Cross-cutting questions that tie all dimensions together |

The scoring engine applies three international learning models — **VARK**, **Felder–Soloman (ILS)**, and **Kolb** — to derive your profile from the raw answers.

## What gets saved

When you submit the quiz, the backend calls `POST /api/diagnostics` and saves a row to the `soft_skills_assessment` table:

```sql
INSERT INTO soft_skills_assessment (
  coder_id,
  autonomy,          -- 1–5
  time_management,   -- 1–5
  problem_solving,   -- 1–5
  communication,     -- 1–5
  teamwork,          -- 1–5
  learning_style,    -- visual | auditory | kinesthetic | mixed
  raw_answers        -- JSONB of all 30 answers
)
```

The five skill scores (each on a 1–5 scale) are **derived on the server** from the tag tallies in your raw answers. The frontend never calculates scores itself.

<Note>
  There are no right or wrong answers. Choose the option that best reflects your natural reaction, not the one you think sounds best.
</Note>

## Score derivation

The backend maps ILS and Kolb answer tags to soft skill scores using psychological mappings:

| Skill | Derived from |
|-------|--------------|
| `autonomy` | REF (reflective) + AC (abstract conceptualization) + RO (reflective observation) |
| `time_management` | SEQ (sequential) + AE (active experimentation) + SNS (sensing) |
| `problem_solving` | GLO (global) + AC + INT (intuitive) |
| `communication` | ACT (active) + CE (concrete experience) + VRB (verbal) |
| `teamwork` | ACT + CE + SNS |

The dominant `learning_style` is set to whichever VARK category accounts for ≥ 35% of your total V/A/R/K answers. If no single style reaches that threshold, it defaults to `mixed`.

## Step-by-step flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in for the first time">
    After a successful login, the server checks `users.first_login`. When it is `true`, you are redirected to `/views/coder/onboarding.html` instead of the dashboard.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Read the block header and answer each question">
    A block badge (e.g., **B1**) and its title appear above the question card. Click the option that best describes you — the next question loads automatically. You can also press **A**, **B**, **C**, or **D** on your keyboard. Use the **← Back** button to change a previous answer.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete all 30 questions">
    A progress bar in the top navigation shows your position (e.g., *B1 · Question 3 of 30 · 10%*). Once you answer the final question, the form is submitted automatically.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Backend scores and saves your assessment">
    The server tallies your answer tags, derives the five skill scores and your `learning_style`, and inserts a row into `soft_skills_assessment`. It immediately responds with `201` and then fires a background request to the Python microservice to generate your first interpretive learning plan.

    ```json
    // POST /api/diagnostics — response
    {
      "message": "Diagnostic saved successfully",
      "planRequested": true,
      "diagnostic": {
        "autonomy": 3,
        "timeManagement": 4,
        "problemSolving": 3,
        "communication": 2,
        "teamwork": 4,
        "learningStyle": "visual",
        "assessedAt": "2026-03-15T14:22:00Z"
      }
    }
    ```
  </Step>

  <Step title="Redirected to your dashboard">
    After submission, you land on the coder dashboard. Your learning plan cards may take a few seconds to appear while the AI generates them in the background.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## API reference

| Method | Endpoint | Description |
|--------|----------|-------------|
| `POST` | `/api/diagnostics` | Submit raw answers; server derives scores and saves assessment |
| `GET` | `/api/diagnostics/me` | Retrieve your existing assessment |

<Warning>
  If you navigate away during the quiz, your progress is not saved. You will need to start from question 1 again.
</Warning>
