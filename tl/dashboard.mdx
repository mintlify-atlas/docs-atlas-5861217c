The Team Leader dashboard is the central hub for monitoring your clan. After login, Kairo loads your clan automatically from the `users` table (`clan_id` column) and populates the entire view with live data for every coder assigned to you.

## Overview stats

The top row of the dashboard surfaces five key numbers at a glance:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Coders in clan" icon="users">
    Total coders in your clan, derived from `users WHERE role = 'coder' AND clan = <your_clan>`.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Onboarding complete" icon="circle-check">
    Coders where `first_login = false` — they have completed the soft-skills quiz.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Pending onboarding" icon="hourglass-half">
    Coders still on `first_login = true` who have not yet completed their assessment.
  </Card>
  <Card title="At risk" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    Coders with an unresolved `risk_flags` record where `risk_level` is `high` or `critical` and `resolved = false`.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Average Kairo score" icon="chart-line">
    Mean `kairo_score` across all active coders in your clan, updated in real time.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Score average (Moodle)" icon="star">
    Mean `average_score` from `moodle_progress`, reflecting academic performance per module.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Coder table

The main table lists every coder in your clan. It is filterable by three quick-access chips:

| Filter | What it shows |
|--------|---------------|
| **All** | Every coder in the clan |
| **At risk** | Coders with an unresolved `risk_flags` record |
| **Pending** | Coders with `first_login = true` |

Each row shows: name, `average_score`, `current_week`, `learning_style`, and risk status badge.

<Info>
  The table is ordered by risk severity first (critical → high → medium → none), then alphabetically by `full_name`. This ensures the coders who need the most attention appear at the top.
</Info>

## Coder detail panel

Clicking any row in the coder table opens the side panel without leaving the page:

- **Identity** — `full_name`, `clan_id`, `email`
- **Academic** — `current_week`, `average_score`
- **Learning style** — `learning_style` from `soft_skills_assessment`
- **Skill bars** — `autonomy`, `time_management`, `problem_solving`, `communication`, `teamwork` visualised as progress bars
- **Risk badge** — shown when the coder has an unresolved high-risk flag
- **Quick feedback** — send a feedback message directly from the panel (see [Sending feedback](/tl/feedback))
- **PDF export** — download a jsPDF summary of the coder's profile

## Exercise submissions section

Below the main content grid, the **Exercises submitted by the clan** section lists the 50 most recent exercise submissions from all coders in your clan, sourced via:

```sql
SELECT es.*, e.title, e.language, e.difficulty, e.expected_output,
       u.full_name, cp.id AS plan_id
FROM exercise_submissions es
JOIN exercises e  ON es.exercise_id = e.id
JOIN users u      ON es.coder_id = u.id
JOIN complementary_plans cp ON e.plan_id = cp.id
WHERE u.clan = $1
ORDER BY es.submitted_at DESC
LIMIT 50
```

Click **Review** on any submission to open the review modal. You can read the submitted code, see the expected output, and write feedback. When you submit, the coder receives +15 Kairo points and a real-time notification.

## Score ranking

The bottom of the dashboard renders two rankings pulled from `GET /api/tl/ranking`:

- **Clan ranking** — all coders in your clan ordered by `kairo_score DESC`
- **Global ranking** — top 10 coders across all clans
